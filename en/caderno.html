Three packages for the exercise

Fictional amounts calculated with combined fees of 10% and a 20% margin. Check your own terms in the calculator.

A · one piece

One 15-second vertical video, one consolidated revision round. Base: R$ 600. Simulated price: R$ 857.14.

B · three pieces

Three 15-second vertical videos, two consolidated revision rounds for the series. Base: R$ 1,170. Simulated price: R$ 1,671.43.

C · five pieces and covers

Five 15-second vertical videos and one cover per video, two revision rounds for the series. Base: R$ 1,740. Simulated price: R$ 2,485.71.

Common to all: script, editing, authorized voice when used, and MP4. Organic use on the brand's channels for 30 days, territory to be defined. Excludes on-site filming, translation, editable files, and paid media. Third-party rights must allow the combined use.

Portfolio in seven days

  1. Define the offer and criteria for the three pieces.
  2. Select existing files and check permissions.
  3. Revise the first piece.
  4. Revise the second piece.
  5. Revise the third piece.
  6. Assemble the PDF, context, and links.
  7. Open everything on your phone and review it for clarity.

Set aside 12–18 hours for the course if you have existing pieces. If you need to create them from scratch, extend the plan. Seven days is a suggested way to organize the work, not a promise of production or a client.
