Calculate costs in reais before choosing a margin

You can put together a simple calculation with identified hours, expenses, and percentages.

Copying a price without knowing your costs can turn a job with many deliverables into a loss.

In 1 minute

  1. Cost: 6 × R$ 60 + R$ 90 + R$ 150 = R$ 600
  2. Fictional allowances: 10% fees and 20% margin
  3. Simulated price: R$ 600 ÷ 0.70 ≈ R$ 857.14

1Your cost needs to include your own work

Fictional example: six hours at R$ 60 add up to R$ 360.

Add R$ 90 in direct expenses and R$ 150 in allocated fixed costs.

The base is R$ 600.

The hourly rate is an exercise assumption, not a professional minimum or a Brazilian average.

One way to plan an hourly rate is to divide your desired monthly pay by the hours you can actually bill.

R$ 2,400 divided by 40 hours comes to R$ 60.

Sales, study, and administrative hours also take up time in your schedule.

Include fixed costs separately so they are not forgotten or counted twice.

Rita includes her work hours in the cost base.

2Margin on price is different from markup on cost

In this simplified model, set aside 10% of the price for taxes and fees and 20% for margin.

Divide R$ 600 by 0.70: approximately R$ 857.14.

Adding only 20% to cost does not produce a 20% margin on the price.

The calculator shows the difference.

The percentages are illustrative.

Check your tax status, fees, and costs before preparing a real estimate.

The calculated remainder represents the result of this model only if all costs are included.

Use the exercise to check the math, without treating the simulation as personal tax guidance.

Separate production from permission to use

You can write a usage worksheet with a term, channels, and limits.

Paying for production does not automatically explain where or how long the material may be used.

In 1 minute

  1. Usage: organic, brand channels, 30 days
  2. Excluded: paid media and unnegotiated exclusivity
  3. Check: image, voice, music, and tools

1Usage rights need to appear in the agreement

Record who may use the material, which material, channels, territory, term, and whether it can be changed.

Organic use means posting without paying to boost distribution.

Boosted posts and paid ads are paid media.

In the Bruma exercise, the proposal allows organic use on the brand's channels for 30 days.

Paid media and exclusivity are excluded until they are negotiated.

Brazil's Copyright Law addresses forms of use and transfer of rights.

Buying a copy does not automatically transfer all economic rights.

Rights assignment contracts require care about form and scope.

Use the official source for general guidance and review a real agreement with a qualified professional.

Do not promise exclusivity on material your license does not allow.
