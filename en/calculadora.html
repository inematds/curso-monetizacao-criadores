How to interpret

Cost = hours × hourly cost + direct expenses + fixed-cost share. Price = cost ÷ (1 − fees − margin), with percentages converted to decimals.

In example A: R$ 600 ÷ 0.70 = approximately R$ 857.14. Adding only 20% to cost would produce a different result. The remainder represents the expected margin only if all relevant costs and fees are included.

Base B: 14 × 60 + 180 + 150 = R$ 1,170. Base C: 22 × 60 + 270 + 150 = R$ 1,740. With the same assumptions, approximate prices are R$ 1,671.43 and R$ 2,485.71. These are budgeting examples, not pricing recommendations.

If fees plus margin reach 100%, the formula has no valid price. Missing, negative, or non-finite values also produce no result.
