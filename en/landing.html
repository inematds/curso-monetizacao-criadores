Before you begin

You need to know how to create images and edit short videos. If needed, first take Ad Lab and Figma for Creators. Use your own files or files you are authorized to use. External tools may have their own costs and limits.

The deliverable requires three real pieces and a reviewed PDF, along with the study proposal. Seven days is a suggested way to organize the portfolio. Production, research, and negotiation take more time than the short exercises.

Simulated amounts and fees are not tax guidance or recommended prices. The public research is dated and compares different terms. Confirm the obligations and rights that apply to your situation before hiring.

The INEMA course is free. No messages, hiring, business, or payment happens through these pages. Preparation does not guarantee income. Reading and activities; no recorded video lessons. Progress stays in this browser; export copies to keep them.
